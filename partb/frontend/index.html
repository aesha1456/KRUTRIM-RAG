About

Krutrim RAG is an internal knowledge-base system built to process and search ISRO's internal documentation engineering documents, reports, and technical manuals. It ingests PDF books, indexes them into a vector store and a knowledge graph, and answers natural-language questions with inline citations and clickable page references all on locally hosted models, fully offline and secure.

Developed by the Payload Check-out & Testing Division (PCTD) at the Checkout Lab, Space Applications Centre (SAC), Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), Ahmedabad.
